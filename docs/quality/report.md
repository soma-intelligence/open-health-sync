# Quality score backtest

Model `2026-10-25.1`. Generated by `OHS_QUALITY_WRITE=1 go test -tags bench ./internal/quality/bench -run TestBench -v`;
see [docs/quality.md](../quality.md) for the method. Leave one subject out: every figure is scored by a residual
model and conformal factors fitted without its subject. Every interval is a 95% subject-cluster bootstrap (400
resamples of people, all their figures with them): figures of one person are autocorrelated, so a pooled count
of figures overstates the evidence. Numbers only; only derived figures enter the repository.

Headline: device timestamps as written (lag 0), as production sees them. The fitted-lag variant, each device's
timestamps shifted by the lag that best matches the reference, is reported beside it for comparison only;
production applies no lag.

Group sizes are counted in subjects as well as figures: a gate group needs 50 figures and 5 subjects; a
calibration group needs 50 figures and 8 subjects for its shift and gamma, 200 and 10 for its own degrees
of freedom, 300 and 10 for its q map, and otherwise falls back to its parent.

sit above the point estimate.
