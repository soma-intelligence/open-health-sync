# Walch et al. 2019: held out evaluation

Model `2026-10-25.1`, as embedded, scored on Walch et al. 2019 (PhysioNet sleep-accel 1.0.0, ODC-By 1.0) without any
fitting on it. Generated by `OHS_QUALITY_WRITE=1 go test -tags bench ./internal/quality/bench -run TestWalchEval -v`.

The figure is total sleep per night from a fixed actigraphy rule on the Apple Watch motion (made by the bench:
the release has no sleep figure from Apple and no ECG), against polysomnography. The shipped calibration for
sleep comes from the synthetic source only, so this is a test of the priors more than of anything learned.
Intervals are 95% bootstraps over nights (one night per person).

Walch et al. 2019: 31 nights. The bench's actigraphy rule on the watch's motion calls 99.5% of PSG sleep epochs sleep (sensitivity) and 17.2% of PSG wake epochs wake (specificity); its total sleep is 28.3 min off on average (SD 17.0) against polysomnography. The release has no ECG, so no heart rate error can be measured from it.
