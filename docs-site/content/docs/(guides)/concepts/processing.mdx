An upload answers as soon as the payload is safe in the raw store. Normalizing it happens in the
background, so "did my data land?" has its own answer: the coverage report.

```sh
curl -s "$OHS/v1/users/$USER_ID/coverage" -H "Authorization: Bearer $KEY"
```

## The rule of thumb

<Steps>
<Step>
After an upload, poll the coverage report until `pending_jobs` is 0.
</Step>
<Step>
Then read. A non-zero `rejected_records` means some lines were not stored; an operator finds
each line and its reason in `ohs_data.failed_records`.
</Step>
<Step>
A non-zero `parked_jobs` is for the operator: those jobs failed 5 times.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Retries and crashes

- A failed job is retried with exponential backoff (30 seconds, doubling) and parked after 5
  attempts.
- A job interrupted by a crash is claimed again after its 10 minute visibility timeout.
- A provider's 429 defers that connection's job until `Retry-After`, without counting a failed
  attempt.
- Records that failed for a recoverable reason are replayed once per process start, so a fixed
  release picks them up after a restart.

What to do after a long outage, and how to re-process a user from the raw files, is in
[Operating notes](/docs/self-hosting/operating-notes).
